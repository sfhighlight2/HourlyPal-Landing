About

Local help should feel safe, simple, and human.

HourlyPal connects people who need short-term help with background-checked local Pals who can offer their time by the hour.

What we are building

HourlyPal is a two-sided marketplace for everyday services like personal assistance, tutoring, personal training, airport chauffeur help, personal styling, green thumb projects, tours, and companionship.

The app is designed for flexible, local bookings without long-term commitments. Clients can search by service, location, and availability. Pals can present their profile, set availability, and respond to booking requests.

How trust works

  • Pals complete a background check before their profile is made available.
  • Profiles show ratings, reviews, services, approximate distance, and verification status.
  • Private alias messaging helps people communicate before they decide what to share.
  • Users can block, report, or ask HourlyPal support to review safety concerns.

Our goal

We want HourlyPal to make local help easier to find while giving independent Pals a cleaner way to offer their time. The product is built around privacy, flexible scheduling, and clear expectations before a booking starts.
